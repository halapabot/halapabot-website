The core idea

Data first.
AI second.

The goal is not to make AI the center of your stream. The goal is to give AI the context it needs to be useful — and to keep the boundary between known information and generated interpretation clear.

How it works

Context before action.

HalapaBot follows a simple chain: understand what the application actually knows, interpret that context, decide whether anything should happen, and only then take an action.

01 — DATA

What is actually happening?

Twitch activity, stream state, configured services and available signals.

02 — CONTEXT

What does it mean here?

Relevant history and current state are considered before a response or action.

03 — DECISION

Should anything happen?

Commands, rules, AI and automation are separate tools for the same stream context.

04 — ACTION

Do the useful thing.

Respond, control, remember, moderate, analyze or prepare content.

The boundary matters

Known data stays known data.

HalapaBot's strongest differentiator is not pretending that generated text is the same thing as observed stream data.

Product principle“HalapaBot only analyzes the data it actually knows about.”

Context is explicit. Missing information is not silently turned into certainty.

Design principleData first. AI second.

AI is a tool inside the control center — not the source of truth for everything that happens on stream.
